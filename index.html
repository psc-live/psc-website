About Physical Sciences Consultorium

Physical Sciences Consultorium (PSC) is an educational and scientific initiative dedicated to exploring, understanding and communicating the wonders of the physical world.

We believe that science should not remain confined to textbooks, laboratories or academic boundaries. Scientific knowledge becomes more meaningful when it is accessible, engaging and shared.

Through educational content, scientific exploration, research-oriented activities and open resources, PSC aims to connect scientific ideas with learners, educators, researchers and curious minds.

Our Mission

To make the physical sciences accessible and engaging through quality educational resources, scientific communication, exploration and research-driven learning.

Our Vision

To cultivate a culture of scientific curiosity, critical thinking and lifelong learning, connecting people with the ideas and discoveries that shape our understanding of the universe.

Our Approach

We bring together conceptual understanding, visual storytelling, computational exploration and interdisciplinary perspectives to make science meaningful and approachable.

Our Focus Areas

  • Physics Education & Science Communication
  • Scientific Research & Exploration
  • Computational Physics & Scientific Computing
  • Open Science & Educational Resources
  • Interdisciplinary Scientific Learning
THE PERSON BEHIND PSC

Meet the Founder

The vision and commitment behind Physical Sciences Consultorium.

FOUNDER, PSC

Lalit Kumar Shukla

Physicist · Educator · Science Communicator

Lalit Kumar Shukla is a physicist, educator and science communicator committed to making scientific knowledge accessible, engaging and meaningful. He holds an M.Sc. in Physics from Banaras Hindu University (BHU) and is CSIR-UGC NET qualified. He also served as a Senior Research Fellow at the Physical Research Laboratory (PRL), Ahmedabad. Since 2018, he has been working as an Assistant Professor of Physics, combining academic teaching with a wider interest in scientific exploration and communication.

He established Physical Sciences Consultorium (PSC) with a vision of creating an accessible platform for scientific learning, interdisciplinary thinking and the wider appreciation of physical sciences. Through educational video series, digital learning resources and explorations of emerging scientific ideas, PSC seeks to connect scientific concepts with curiosity, creativity and real-world understanding.
